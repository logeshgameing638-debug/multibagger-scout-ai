How this works

Every weekday evening (IST), a scheduled research run on a free hosted runner scans a bounded batch of the NSE + BSE universe with eight deterministic research agents - fundamental, technical, business quality, valuation, news, risk, scoring and decision. It refreshes the candidate ranking, writes narrative reports for the top candidates, and raises market-derived alerts. Results land on this site within minutes of each run, with the run's own timestamps.

This is scheduled batch research, not continuous monitoring. Every stock keeps its own data date; missing data is shown, never invented. Scores are explained; missing evidence earns nothing.

Research only. No trades are placed by this system; there is no broker connection and no real money. A "multibagger" label is a research hypothesis, not a prediction or advice. Free data can be delayed, incomplete or wrong.
